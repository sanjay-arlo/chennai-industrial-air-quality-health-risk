# Chennai Industrial Air Quality & Health-Risk Analysis

> Business Analyst portfolio case study comparing industrial and residential exposure signals to prioritise air-quality interventions.

## 🚀 Live Dashboard

**[Open the interactive dashboard](https://sanjay-arlo.github.io/chennai-industrial-air-quality-health-risk/)**

## Business question

**Which Chennai zones experience the highest particulate exposure, and where should mitigation effort be prioritised?**

## Dashboard

Explore zone AQI, PM2.5 exposure, unhealthy-day frequency, population-weighted exposure and industrial-versus-residential comparisons.

## KPI logic

**PM2.5 exposure ratio** = observed PM2.5 / selected reference limit.

**Unhealthy-day share** = days at or above the selected unhealthy threshold / days observed × 100.

## Dataset

Synthetic/illustrative zone-day observations are used for portfolio demonstration. They are not official monitoring-station measurements.

## Business Analyst focus

The project demonstrates stakeholder framing, exposure segmentation, KPI governance, prioritisation and recommendation design rather than medical diagnosis.

## Production upgrade

Integrate governed CPCB/TNPCB monitoring data, station metadata, meteorology, population grids and industrial-source inventories. Add QA flags, missing-data treatment, source lineage and alert thresholds approved by domain teams.

## Limitation

Portfolio case study only; exposure indicators should not be interpreted as individual medical risk.
